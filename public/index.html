      How to play

      1. You get a question on an exit sign, worded in fancy vocabulary (Cite a savory garnish customarily strewn atop a baked, saucy disc of cheesy dough means a pizza topping). Decode it first. The plain version is shown after you answer. An obstacle appears far down the road, and it gets closer as the clock runs down.
      2. Type the answer most people would give before it reaches you. Typos are forgiven.
      3. Score anything and you swerve clear. Run out of time, or give an answer that doesn't fit, and you crash into it. Every crash costs $25. When your run ends you watch your winnings roll in, then your car is repaired automatically and the repair bill is taken out of your total.
      4. The more popular your answer, the more cash you earn. Only the #1 answer (pepperoni) is a John Doe: $100. The next 5 are Average Joes ($85), the next 10 Neighbors ($60), the next 20 Strangers ($30). Any other real answer is an Alien: $10.
      5. Your cash adds up every day. Spend it in the Shop (top right) on better cars. Cars are sorted by answer level: cheap starters are Aliens, and the absurd Big Green Tractor is a John Doe.
      6. Keep a streak. Play two or more days in a row and each day starts with a cash bonus for that day's earnings (it grows every day, up to +50%) and a mystery box. A box holds a puzzle piece for one level of cars; collect enough pieces for a car and it's yours for free. Pricier levels have rarer pieces.

      Ten questions a day, with a new set at midnight UTC. A perfect day is $1,000.
